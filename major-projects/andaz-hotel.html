PROJECT OVERVIEW

Andaz Hotel

Andaz Hotel is a major hospitality development located in Aerocity, New Delhi.

PRUF Engineering & Consultants was associated with the project through an extensive multidisciplinary scope covering structural construction, block work, water bodies, external development, landscape works, infrastructure, stone and road works, waterproofing and finishing works.

01

Location

Aerocity
New Delhi

Located within Aerocity, one of New Delhi's prominent hospitality and commercial districts near Indira Gandhi International Airport.

02

Scope of Work

Structure, Block Work, Water Bodies, External Development, Hardscape & Softscape, Substation, HSD Tank, Stone Work, Road Work, Terrace Waterproofing & Kitchen-Area Finishes

PRUF Engineering & Consultants' involvement extended across multiple stages and disciplines of the project, combining core building construction with external development, infrastructure and architectural finishing works.
